# Afterimage — Image Generation Prompts

Paste-ready prompts for Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Stable Diffusion XL, and Flux.
Each prompt aims to produce a premium product render of Afterimage as if it
were a real iOS app being held / sitting on a real surface. They share a
consistent design language so renders look like the same product.

**Universal style notes** (the engine should infer these from each prompt):
- Phone: iPhone 17 Pro in space black titanium, dynamic island visible.
- App background inside the screen: warm charcoal `#0E0B0A` (never true black).
- Accent: tarnished gold `#C8A96B` and aged Leica brass tones.
- Identity colors only: warm charcoal, muted cream, tarnished gold, film red.
- Texture: subtle film grain on the app UI itself, never on the surrounding environment.
- Lighting: dim, cinematic, single warm light source. Wong Kar-wai grading.
- Grading reference: HBO *Euphoria* title cards, Kodak Portra 400 stock.
- No neon, no purple, no startup blue.
- Mockup discipline: phone frame is present but the screen content is the focus,
  not the device. Margins around the phone are even and tasteful.

## Style Variants

If a render comes out too sterile, append one of these to any prompt:

- `, slight motion blur on hand, candid moment, lived-in`
- `, dust particles in air, single warm bedroom lamp, late-night mood`
- `, shot on Fujifilm GFX 100, 80mm lens, f/2.8, ISO 400`
- `, with a tiny analog timestamp reading "5:42 PM 05-22-2026" subtly visible in the corner like a film print`

If a render comes out too dark to see the UI clearly:
- `, screen brightness dialed up, screen is the brightest object in the composition, soft fill light on phone bezels`

If the device frame dominates the composition (banned per the imagegen skill):
- `, the focus is the app screen content not the phone device, even tasteful margins around the phone, mockup framing discipline`
